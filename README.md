# GPU Hotel

A cute browser game about how AI clouds make money. You run a hotel with 100 rooms: rooms are GPUs, guests are requests to AI models, and getting a room ready is loading a model onto a GPU.

Play online: https://wyy511511.github.io/GPU-hotel/ (once GitHub Pages is on). Or open `index.html` in a browser; there is no build step.

Languages: English, Español, Français, Türkçe, 中文 (picker in the top-right corner).

## Easy · The Hotel

For anyone, no tech knowledge needed. Three kinds of guests (chatters, photographers, moviemakers) with different stays, room sharing and patience. Keep enough rooms ready that guests don't walk out, but not so many that you pay rent for empty rooms. Seven days of events (a premiere night, a conference, a power cut, a heat wave, a big customer leaving), each ending with a one-line "In AI terms" explanation, plus a glossary that maps every game idea to its AI equivalent.

## Pro · Model Marketplace

How a fal-style platform works: one API key that sells many labs' image, video and audio models.

- **Open models** run in your own rooms and you keep the whole price; **closed models** run in the lab's rooms and you keep only its cut, under a lab rate limit.
- **Customers pick a model, not a hotel.** If a model isn't on your shelf, most of its demand goes elsewhere (Coverage).
- **Labs pick launch partners by developer community**; exclusive turbo versions can't be pulled from you.
- Named customers complain, leave, or try to graduate to their own GPUs.
- Day 7 ends with a valuation that rewards community, exclusive models and margin, and punishes dependence on one customer.

Model, lab and customer names are fictional; the sources table in the game maps them to real-world parallels. Prices, traffic and event strength are tuned for play and are not real quotes.

`legacy/` keeps the earlier versions.
